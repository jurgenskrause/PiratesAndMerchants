Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production sprite atlas for a top-down watercolor hex maritime game matching hand-painted tan wood roads and muted teal seas. Exactly 3 columns and 2 rows of equal square cells. Each sprite is a regular POINTY-TOP hex, opaque watercolor blue-green water covering the exact hex and transparent outside. Flat orthographic overhead view, no camera perspective, no land, no ships, no text, no shadows outside hex. Each pier runs horizontally from the exact midpoint of the WEST hex edge toward the EAST. Connection width identical at shared edges. Row 1 left: small wooden dock, shoreward walkway enters WEST midpoint and ends as a small broad rounded terminal platform just right of center; sufficient visual room on both sides. Row 1 middle: medium dock root tile, wooden walkway enters WEST midpoint and exits EAST midpoint, crates and posts on narrow edges; row 1 right: medium dock seaward tip tile, walkway enters WEST midpoint and ends in broad cargo platform right of center, three bollards. Row 2 left: deep-water quay root tile, wider reinforced timber and stone causeway from exact WEST midpoint to exact EAST midpoint; row 2 middle: matching deep-water middle tile, same width through WEST and EAST midpoint, heavy posts; row 2 right: deep-water seaward tip, enters WEST midpoint and ends as a broad reinforced terminal wharf to the right, large bollards, sturdy stone face. Preserve clearly different visual sizes for small, medium, deep-water; matching root/middle/tip edges. Smooth muted painted water without tiny distracting noise. Every sprite centered in its own equal square frame, matching identical hex dimensions. Genuine transparency outside every hex, no labels, no border lines. Six sprite frames only. Each hex vertices normalized in its frame (0.5,0.02),(0.915692,0.26),(0.915692,0.74),(0.5,0.98),(0.084308,0.74),(0.084308,0.26). West entry=(0.084308,0.5), East exit=(0.915692,0.5). Geometry and aligned edge centers are mandatory.

Mode: built-in image generation, new atlas.
Final asset: dist/gfx/docks/painted-docks-v1.png.
Source UV registration is stored on the six dock tile assets in assets.json. Each full tile is mapped onto an exact regular gameplay hex, with west/east pier entry registration, and receives filtered mipmaps. Multi-tile docks select root, middle and tip artwork and rotate it with the world-facing direction.
