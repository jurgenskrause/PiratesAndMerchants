# Default market initialization and trading

The TRADEWINDS 256 default world reproduced the report: at navigation tick 800 it had zero funded purchase orders, zero accepted trades and zero completed deliveries. Ships were exploring normally. Every port was initialized with a full town buffer plus 30–60 ordinary market units of every cargo type, multiplied further for larger colonies. At the existing consumption rate of 0.1 units per economic update, each town replenished itself from its own market for thousands of navigation ticks. No external buyer demand was created.

The settlement catalog now sets ordinary opening market stock and its ordinary variation step to zero. Opening specialty stock and stock for actual local primary producers remain configured at their existing values. Town buffers, colony multipliers, production and consumption rates, prices, treasury grants and merchant capital remain unchanged. Non-specialist ports now procure missing goods through funded orders as their initial internal stores are consumed. This is an explicit change to initial goods, not ongoing stock removal, an automatic buyer grant or new pricing.

The generated-world regression uses the active BridgeLogistics and SimulationRuntime with default configuration, six merchants, military patrols and no stockpile modifiers or extra treasury funding. It verifies completed purchases and deliveries by tick 800, whole-unit pickups, exact total microgold conservation and goods changes explained by recorded production and consumption. A diagnostic run completed 30 deliveries and recorded 61 accepted trades by tick 800; scheduling can vary these counts.

Manufacturing placement and further demand/production tuning remain separate development work. This change does not activate disabled factory placement or introduce new production chains. Existing ordinary-stock catalog controls can still seed general warehouse stock if intentionally configured, but defaults no longer make all settlements self-sufficient.
